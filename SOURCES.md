## Lead: First Pakistani in orbit

- China's Shenzhou-24 crewed spaceship launched on Wednesday, 25 November
  2026, at 8:18 a.m. Beijing time (00:18 UTC) from the Jiuquan Satellite
  Launch Center aboard a Long March 2F rocket.
- Crew: commander Tang Hongbo (his third visit to Tiangong), flight
  engineer Wu Yi (first flight), and Pakistani payload specialist
  Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, 40.
- Ali is the first Pakistani in orbit and the first foreign astronaut ever
  to visit China's Tiangong space station. He will spend about a week
  aboard the station running experiments, then return on Shenzhou-23.
- Sources: Reuters, "China's Shenzhou-24 space flight takes off with
  Pakistani national as first foreign astronaut", 25 November 2026;
  Xinhua via China Military / People's Daily, 25 November 2026;
  Wikipedia, "Shenzhou 24" (launch date 25 November 2026, 00:18:52 UTC).

## Solomon Islands signs pact with Australia

- The prime ministers of Australia (Anthony Albanese) and Solomon Islands
  (Matthew Wale) signed a bilateral security treaty in Canberra on
  Wednesday, 25 November 2026.
- The treaty names Australia the Solomons' primary security partner and
  states there will be no foreign military or security base on the islands.
  It follows reports that the Solomons will end its 2022 security pact
  with China when it expires in April.
- Sources: Associated Press, "Australia and Solomon Islands sign a
  security pact that excludes China", 25 November 2026; The Times,
  "Blow for China as Solomon Islands scraps security pact with Beijing",
  25 November 2026.

- Source: Reuters, "Stocks retreat as AI rebound stumbles, tariff threats

## US inflation test lands before Thanksgiving

- The US releases its October PCE inflation report and the second estimate
  of third-quarter GDP on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, at 8:30 a.m. ET,
  the day before Thanksgiving.
- Core PCE is forecast to rise 0.3% month on month and 3.2% year on year,
  up from 3.1%; headline PCE is forecast at 3.8% year on year, up from
  3.6%. The data could shape the Federal Reserve's December decision.
- Sources: Reuters markets coverage, 25 November 2026; Barron's,
  "Stubborn Inflation Could Pose a Bigger Threat to Markets Than AI
  Worries", 25 November 2026; Morningstar / Dow Jones data calendar,
  24 November 2026.
